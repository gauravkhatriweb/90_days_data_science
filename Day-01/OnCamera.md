ON-CAMERA SHOTS GUIDE
=====================

SHOT 1 — Opening Hook
---------------------
Location: Desk, sitting position
What to say: "90 din. Data Scientist banna hai. 
Aur aaj pehla din hai... aur mujhe abhi variables 
samajh nahi aa rahe."

Facial expression: Slight confusion mixed with determination
Eyes: Look down briefly at 0:01, then slowly raise to camera
Energy: Low, honest, vulnerable
Eye contact: Direct after the initial look down
Pause: 1 second pause after "pehla din hai..."
Tone: Quiet, almost whispering

SHOT 2 — Morning Energy
-----------------------
Location: Same desk or standing near window
What to say: "Subah uthte hi socha tha — aaj coding 
start karenge, energy full thi."

Facial expression: Slight smile, remembering morning
Energy: Slightly higher, nostalgic
Eye contact: Direct, warm
Tone: Hopeful

SHOT 3 — The Crash
------------------
Location: Same position
What to say: "Lekin video chalayi, 10 minute mein 
samajh aaya ke... yeh toh koi aasan rasta nahi hai."

Facial expression: Reality hitting, smile fades
Energy: Drops, becomes serious
Eye contact: Look away briefly at "samajh aaya"
Pause: Beat before "yeh toh koi aasan rasta nahi hai"
Tone: Honest, slightly defeated but not broken

SHOT 4 — The Climb Back
-----------------------
Location: Desk, leaning forward slightly
What to say: "Variables, loops, functions, lists, 
dictionaries... ek saath sab aaya toh dimaag ghoom gaya. 
Lekin phir ek cheez ki — slowly karo. Ek ek karke."

Facial expression: Concentrating, then calming down
Energy: Building back up
Eye contact: Direct during "slowly karo"
Hand gesture: Small hand movement on "ek ek karke"
Tone: Determined, self-talk

SHOT 5 — The Win
----------------
Location: Same, maybe slightly different angle
What to say: "Aur jab pehli dafa ek loop chala kar 
output aaya screen pe... woh feel alag hi thi. 
Chhoti si baat hai lekin... kuch ban raha hai."

Facial expression: Genuine small smile, pride
Energy: Warm, satisfied
Eye contact: Soft, reflective
Pause: 1 second before "chhoti si baat hai"
Tone: Quiet pride, almost emotional

SHOT 6 — End of Day
-------------------
Location: Same desk, maybe darker lighting
What to say: "6 ghante padhai ki aaj. Thoda thak gaya hun. 
Lekin 89 din baaki hain. Kal functions aur 
practice problems hain."

Facial expression: Tired but content
Energy: Low, calm, peaceful
Eye contact: Direct but soft
Tone: Exhausted honesty

SHOT 7 — Closing Hope
---------------------
Location: Close-up, tighter frame
What to say: "Kya pata... 90 din baad main yahan se 
keh sakun ke — haan, main data scientist ban gaya."

Facial expression: Hopeful, slight smile
Energy: Quiet confidence
Eye contact: Direct, intense
Pause: Small pause before "haan"
Tone: Dreamy, forward-looking
