EDITING GUIDE — Day 1
======================

SOFTWARE RECOMMENDATION
-----------------------
Primary: CapCut (mobile) or DaVinci Resolve (desktop)
Alternative: Premiere Pro, Final Cut

PROJECT SETTINGS
----------------
Aspect Ratio: 9:16 (Vertical)
Resolution: 1080 x 1920
Frame Rate: 30fps
Export Bitrate: 15-25 Mbps
Codec: H.264

OPENING PACING (0-5 seconds)
----------------------------
- Start with BLACK FRAME for 0.3 seconds
- Hard cut to face (Shot 01)
- NO music for first 2 seconds — let voice land
- At 0:02, music starts at very low volume
- First line must be clean, no competing audio
- Cut on "hai..." pause — hold on face for beat
- Then continue dialogue
- Total pacing: Slow, deliberate, weighty

CUT TIMING RULES
----------------
- Cut on emotion shifts, not mid-sentence
- When listing topics (variables, loops, functions), 
  cut ON each word to B-roll of that topic
- Hold on emotional beats 0.5-1 second longer than comfortable
- Never cut during a pause — let pauses breathe

RETENTION STRATEGY
------------------
0-5 sec: Vulnerability hook (done in script)
5-15 sec: Pattern interrupt — cut from face to B-roll
15-25 sec: Story question — "will they understand?"
25-38 sec: Payoff — first win (emotional reward)
38-50 sec: Tension return — "89 days left"
50-55 sec: Hope — reason to return tomorrow

WHEN TO ZOOM
------------
- Slow push in during hook (Shot 01) — 1-2% over 5 seconds
- Slow push in during closing (Shot 07) — builds intimacy
- Quick zoom punch (105%) on "output aaya" — emphasis
- NO other zooms — keep it clean

WHEN TO SPEED RAMP
-------------------
- Typing B-roll: 0.5x slow → 1x normal (subtle)
- Coffee pick up: 0.5x slow motion
- NO fast speed ramps today — day 1 should feel heavy, deliberate

WHERE TO SLOW DOWN
------------------
- After "dimaag ghoom gaya" — hold 1 second on face
- On "kuch ban raha hai" — let it land, 1.5 second hold
- Final line — slow, deliberate, fade

PUNCH-INS
---------
- During "yeh toh koi aasan rasta nahi hai" — 
  punch in 110% on face for emphasis
- During "kuch ban raha hai" — subtle punch in

JUMP CUTS
---------
- Use sparingly
- OK to jump cut within same take if removing dead air
- Do NOT jump cut for energy — that feels fake
- If jump cutting, shift frame slightly (not same position)

HARD CUTS
---------
- Primary transition method
- Use for: B-roll inserts, topic changes, time jumps
- No fancy transitions needed — hard cuts feel documentary

MUSIC TIMING
------------
0:00-0:02 — SILENCE (let hook land)
0:02-0:10 — Music fades in at 15% volume
0:10-0:25 — Music at 20%, steady
0:25-0:35 — Music dips to 10% during win moment
0:35-0:45 — Music returns to 20%
0:45-0:55 — Music fades out over last 5 seconds
0:55 — Silence + "Day 1 complete" text

COLOR GRADING
-------------
Style: Warm documentary, slightly desaturated
- Temperature: Slightly warm (shift +5-10)
- Tint: Neutral to slightly green
- Contrast: Medium-low (not punchy)
- Highlights: Pull down slightly (-10)
- Shadows: Lift slightly (+10)
- Saturation: -10 to -15
- Vignette: Subtle, darkened edges

Look reference: Netflix documentary warmth
Not: Bright vlog, not moody dark, not film emulation

SOUND LAYERING
--------------
Layer 1: Voice (primary, cleanest)
Layer 2: Music (background, controlled)
Layer 3: Ambient room tone (very subtle, constant)
Layer 4: Sound effects (keyboard, clicks — synced)
Layer 5: Silence (used strategically)

SILENCE MOMENTS
---------------
- 0:00-0:02 — Complete silence before hook
- After "kuch ban raha hai" — 0.5 second silence
- 0:53-0:55 — Music fades, brief silence before text
- Silence creates weight — use it 3-4 times max

MOTION BLUR
-----------
- NOT recommended for this style
- Keep everything clean
- If any motion blur, only on speed ramped footage

CAPTIONS/SUBTITLES
-------------------
Style: Clean, modern, readable
Font: Inter, SF Pro, or Montserrat (Bold)
Size: Large enough for mobile (equivalent to 42-48px)
Color: White with black outline/shadow
Position: Center or lower third
Animation: Word-by-word highlight (karaoke style)
- Highlighted word: Yellow (#FFD700) or accent color
- Non-highlighted: White
Timing: Perfectly synced to speech
Max words per line: 3-4
Safe area: Keep within 90% center (avoid edge cutoff)

SAFE AREAS
----------
- Keep all important elements within center 80%
- Text: Lower third, centered horizontally
- Face: Upper center
- Avoid putting anything in top/bottom 10%

EXPORT SETTINGS
---------------
Format: MP4
Codec: H.264
Resolution: 1080x1920
Frame Rate: 30fps
Bitrate: 15-25 Mbps (Variable)
Audio: AAC, 320kbps, Stereo
Audio Normalization: -3dB to -1dB peak
