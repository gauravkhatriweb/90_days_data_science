THUMBNAIL GUIDE — Day 1
========================

FACIAL EXPRESSION
-----------------
Primary: Concerned but determined
- Slight furrow in brows
- Eyes focused
- Mouth slightly open (mid-speech)
- NOT fully smiling (too happy for hook)
- NOT fully sad (too negative)

Secondary option: The small proud smile from Shot 05
- Use only if A/B testing happier thumbnail

OBJECT TO HOLD (Optional)
-------------------------
- Laptop partially visible in frame
- OR coffee mug (relatable)
- OR nothing (cleaner, more focus on face)
- Recommendation: Nothing — let face carry it

TEXT ON THUMBNAIL
-----------------
Option A: "DAY 1" (large, top or bottom)
Option B: "90 DIN" (large, side)
Option C: "DATA SCIENTIST?" (question creates curiosity)
Option D: "SHURU?" (minimal, intriguing)

Recommended: "DAY 1" in bold white + small subtitle "Data Scientist?"

Text placement: 
- "DAY 1" — Top left or bottom left
- Subtitle — Under main text, smaller
- Keep right side clear for face

Text style:
- Bold, thick font
- White with black outline
- Slightly tilted (-3 to -5 degrees) for energy
- Large enough to read on mobile

BACKGROUND
----------
- Desk/laptop visible but blurred
- Dark background preferred (makes face pop)
- If bright, darken in editing
- No cluttered background
- No other people

COMPOSITION
-----------
- Face: Right third or center-right
- Text: Left side
- Looking space: Face looking toward text or camera
- Rule of thirds applied
- Face takes 40-50% of frame

LIGHTING
--------
- Even lighting on face
- No harsh shadows
- Slight catch light in eyes
- Background slightly darker
- If possible, rim light on one side

COLORS
------
- Warm tones (slight orange/amber)
- High contrast between face and background
- Text: Pure white
- Avoid blue/cold tones
- Slightly saturated but not unnatural

CTR SCORE PREDICTION
--------------------
Estimated CTR: 7.5/10

Why not higher:
- Day 1 has no established audience yet
- "Day 1" is strong for series but weak for search
- No celebrity or extreme visual

Why this high:
- Face creates connection
- "DAY 1" creates series curiosity
- Expression creates emotional hook
- Clean, professional look
- Question element creates click urge

A/B TEST SUGGESTIONS
---------------------
Test A: Concerned face + "DAY 1"
Test B: Confused face + "90 DIN?"
Test C: Slight smile + "SHURU KIYA"

THUMBNAIL DIMENSIONS
--------------------
- Resolution: 1080 x 1920 (same as video)
- Or create 1280 x 720 and crop for Shorts shelf
- Export as JPEG, high quality
- File size: Under 500KB if possible

CREATION METHOD
---------------
1. Screenshot best frame from footage
2. Import to Photoshop, Canva, or Snapseed
3. Crop to 9:16
4. Adjust brightness/contrast
5. Add text with outline
6. Add subtle vignette (darken edges)
7. Export
