TRANSITION GUIDE — Day 1
========================

PRIMARY TRANSITION: HARD CUT
-----------------------------
Used for: 90% of cuts
Why: Documentary feel, authentic, no distraction
How: Simple cut from one clip to next
No effects, no animation, no sound

SECONDARY TRANSITION: MATCH CUT
--------------------------------
Used for: 1-2 times maximum
Example: End of sentence on face → Start of related B-roll
Why: Creates visual connection
How: Match movement or position between shots

SPECIFIC TRANSITIONS BY CUT
---------------------------

Cut 1: Black → Face (Hook)
Transition: Hard cut
Why: Immediate, jarring in a good way
No fade, no animation

Cut 2: Face → Face (Morning line)
Transition: Hard cut or very subtle dissolve (0.3s)
Why: Time has passed (morning to now)
Keep it fast

Cut 3: Face → B-roll (Topic listing starts)
Transition: Hard cut
Why: Pattern interrupt, visual variety

Cut 4: B-roll → B-roll (Each topic)
Transition: Hard cut ON each word
Why: Rhythmic, engaging, shows variety
"variables" CUT "loops" CUT "functions" CUT

Cut 5: B-roll → Face (Recovery line)
Transition: Hard cut
Why: Return to human, emotional landing

Cut 6: Face → B-roll (Code working)
Transition: Hard cut
Why: Show the win visually

Cut 7: B-roll → Face (Win dialogue)
Transition: Hard cut
Why: Back to emotion

Cut 8: Face → Face (End of day)
Transition: Subtle dissolve (0.5s) OR hard cut with lighting change
Why: Time has passed, mood shift
If possible, actually film this later in day with different light

Cut 9: Face → Close-up (Final line)
Transition: Hard cut
Why: Intimacy increase, final statement

Cut 10: Face → Black (End)
Transition: Fade to black (1 second)
Why: Closure, clean ending
Only fade used in entire video

TRANSITIONS TO AVOID
--------------------
❌ Whip pan (too vloggy)
❌ Mask transition (too trendy)
❌ Zoom transition (too flashy)
❌ Glitch (wrong mood)
❌ Slide (amateur feel)
❌ Ink/watercolor (doesn't fit)
❌ Any preset transition from app

WHY MINIMAL TRANSITIONS
------------------------
- Documentary = observational, not stylized
- Hard cuts feel like real life
- Fancier transitions break trust
- Audience should focus on story, not editing
- Day 1 sets the style for 90 days
- Keep it consistent throughout series
