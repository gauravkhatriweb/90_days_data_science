BACKGROUND MUSIC — Day 1
========================

MOOD
----
Contemplative, slightly heavy, hopeful undertone
Not sad, not happy, not energetic
Think: "beginning of a journey" feeling

GENRE
-----
- Ambient piano
- Lo-fi instrumental
- Documentary underscore
- Minimal acoustic

ENERGY LEVEL
------------
Low to Medium-Low
Should support, never lead
Viewer should barely notice it

TEMPO
-----
60-80 BPM
Slow enough to feel heavy
Fast enough to not feel stuck

SPECIFIC TRACK SUGGESTIONS
--------------------------
Search terms for YouTube Audio Library / Epidemic Sound / Artlist:
- "Documentary piano"
- "Ambient contemplative"
- "Minimal piano emotional"
- "Journey beginning instrumental"
- "Lo-fi study ambient"

If using free sources:
- YouTube Audio Library → "Sunrise" by Sid Acharya
- YouTube Audio Library → "Wistful" or "Reflecting"
- Incompetech → "Light Thoughts" or "Porches and Universes"

MUSIC TIMING BREAKDOWN
----------------------
0:00-0:02 — NO MUSIC (silence)
0:02-0:05 — Fade in from 0 to 15% volume
0:05-0:10 — Hold at 15%
0:10-0:15 — Rise to 20%
0:15-0:25 — Hold at 20%
0:25-0:30 — Dip to 10% (win moment coming)
0:30-0:38 — Hold at 10% (let voice be hero)
0:38-0:45 — Rise back to 20%
0:45-0:50 — Begin fade out
0:50-0:55 — Fade from 20% to 0%
0:55+ — Silence

WHEN MUSIC SHOULD BE LOUDER
----------------------------
- During B-roll montages (0:10-0:20)
- When listing topics with cuts

WHEN MUSIC SHOULD BE QUIETER
-----------------------------
- Hook (first 2 seconds — silent)
- Win moment (0:30-0:38) — let emotion breathe
- Final line (0:48-0:55) — voice carries everything

WHEN BEAT DROPS (if track has beats)
-------------------------------------
- Avoid tracks with heavy beats
- If subtle beat exists, it can land on cuts
- NO beat drops — this isn't hype content

WHEN SILENCE SHOULD BE USED
----------------------------
- Opening 2 seconds (critical for hook)
- 0.5 second after "kuch ban raha hai"
- Final 2 seconds before text card
- Total silence: ~5 seconds in 55 second video

ALTERNATIVE MUSIC APPROACH
--------------------------
If you find a track with 2 sections:
- Section A (minimal): 0:02-0:30
- Section B (slight build): 0:30-0:50
- Then fade out

This creates natural arc without manual volume automation
