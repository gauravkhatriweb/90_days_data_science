# 🧭 Personal Brand Guide

## Core Identity
- **Who you are:** A dedicated, relentless learner transforming into a Data Scientist.
- **Tagline:** "90 Days. No shortcuts. No excuses. Just relentless execution."
- **The Vibe:** Honest, humble, hard-working, and highly disciplined.

## Tone of Voice
- **Authentic:** Never fake knowing everything. If a bug took you 4 hours, say it took 4 hours.
- **Action-Oriented:** Focus on *doing* rather than just *studying*. (e.g., "I built X" instead of "I read about X").
- **Encouraging:** Inspire others by showing that consistency > natural talent.

## Visual Identity
- **Colors:** Keep it clean. Dark mode IDEs, green GitHub contribution squares, white/clean text overlays.
- **Pacing:** Fast, snappy, no dead air. Jump cuts are your friend.
- **The "Hero" Shot:** You at your desk, intensely focused, or holding a cup of coffee while code runs.

## Platform Strategy
1. **YouTube Shorts (Primary):** Daily vlog-style updates. High energy, heavy editing, fast pacing.
2. **LinkedIn (Secondary):** Professional reflections. Share your daily GitHub commits, project screenshots, and business value learned.
3. **Twitter/X (Tertiary):** Quick thoughts, raw frustrations, "Today I Learned" snippets, and networking with other devs.
