## Metrics to aim for:
- **Viewed vs Swiped Away:** Aim for > 60% Viewed.
- **Average View Duration (AVD):** Aim for > 70% of the video length (e.g., 42 seconds on a 60-second video).
- If your "Viewed" percentage is low, your **Hook** needs work.
- If your "AVD" is low, your **Pacing/Storytelling** is boring in the middle.
