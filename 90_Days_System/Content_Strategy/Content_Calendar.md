# 📅 90-Day Content Calendar Overview

While you have detailed daily plans (`Day_01_Content.md` - `Day_90_Content.md`), this document provides a high-level view of the overarching narrative themes and milestone projects.

## Month 1: "The Beginner's Struggle" (Days 1-28)
**Goal:** Build relatability. Show the raw struggle of learning syntax, setting up environments, and the joy of small wins.
- **Week 1 (Days 1-7):** Python Basics. *Milestone:* Contact Book App.
- **Week 2 (Days 8-14):** Advanced Python & OOP. *Milestone:* Student Grade System.
- **Week 3 (Days 15-21):** Statistics & Probability. *Milestone:* Probability Simulator.
- **Week 4 (Days 22-28):** Visualizations. *Milestone:* Titanic Mini EDA.

## Month 2: "The Data Wrangler" (Days 29-56)
**Goal:** Show competence. Transition from writing basic scripts to analyzing messy, real-world data and writing complex SQL.
- **Week 5 (Days 29-35):** Pandas & Data Cleaning. *Milestone:* Messy Dataset Cleanup.
- **Week 6 (Days 36-42):** Advanced EDA & SQL Intro. *Milestone:* Full EDA Project.
- **Week 7 (Days 43-49):** Advanced SQL. *Milestone:* E-commerce Analytics Dashboard.
- **Week 8 (Days 50-56):** ML Intro & Regression. *Milestone:* House Price Predictor.

## Month 3: "The Transformation" (Days 57-90)
**Goal:** Prove expertise. Build impressive, portfolio-worthy machine learning and deep learning applications.
- **Week 9 (Days 57-63):** ML Classification. *Milestone:* Spam Classifier / MNIST.
- **Week 10 (Days 64-70):** Unsupervised ML & Tuning. *Milestone:* First Kaggle Entry.
- **Week 11 (Days 71-77):** Neural Networks (ANN). *Milestone:* House Price ANN.
- **Week 12 (Days 78-84):** CNN & RNN. *Milestone:* Image Classification Web App.
- **Week 13 (Days 85-90):** Specialization & Deployment. *Milestone:* Final Deployed Capstone & Portfolio.
