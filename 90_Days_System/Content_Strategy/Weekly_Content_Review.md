# 📊 Weekly Content Review Ritual

Do this every Sunday during your "Weekly Workflow" review to optimize your content strategy.

## 1. The Numbers Check
- **Best Performing Video (Views):** Day ___
- **Highest Retention Rate:** Day ___
- **Most Comments:** Day ___

## 2. The Creative Autopsy
- **Why did the best video work?** (Was the hook better? Was the project visual? Was it highly relatable?)
  - *Notes:* ___________________
- **Why did the worst video flop?** (Too technical? Boring hook? Bad audio?)
  - *Notes:* ___________________

## 3. The "Next Week" Pivot
- What storytelling technique will I try this week?
- Are my videos getting too repetitive? How will I change the scenery or angles?
- Am I showing enough of my actual personality?

## 4. Brand Check-in
- Did I post to LinkedIn/Twitter consistently?
- Is my GitHub remaining green?
- Is my README updated with my latest projects?
