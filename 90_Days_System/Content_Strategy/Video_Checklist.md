# ✅ Daily Video Production Checklist

Use this checklist every day to ensure your Shorts are high quality and consistent.

## Pre-Shoot (Morning)
- [ ] Review today's `Content Plan` (Day_XX_Content.md).
- [ ] Pick the Hook and Story Arc for the day.
- [ ] Clean off desk / set up lighting.

## Shooting (Throughout the Day)
- [ ] **B-Roll 1:** Opening the laptop / starting the timer.
- [ ] **B-Roll 2:** Screen recording of the code/project (use OBS or QuickTime).
- [ ] **B-Roll 3:** A moment of frustration or deep thought.
- [ ] **B-Roll 4:** The "Aha!" moment or project working successfully.
- [ ] **A-Roll (Talking Head):** Deliver the hook (do 3 takes).
- [ ] **A-Roll (Talking Head):** Deliver the daily reflection/lesson (do 2 takes).

## Post-Production (Evening - 30-45 mins max)
- [ ] Import all footage to your editor (CapCut, Premiere, etc.).
- [ ] Cut out all dead air, breaths, and pauses ("Franken-biting").
- [ ] Assemble the Story Arc: Hook -> Context -> Struggle -> Win -> Outro.
- [ ] Add Text Overlays (Captions for the entire video + big text for key concepts).
- [ ] Add subtle sound effects (keyboard typing, whoosh for transitions, pop for UI elements).
- [ ] Export in 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical format), 60fps if possible.

## Uploading
- [ ] Upload to YouTube Shorts.
- [ ] Paste Title, Description, and Hashtags from the Daily Content Plan.
- [ ] Pin a comment with the "Engagement Question" from the plan.
