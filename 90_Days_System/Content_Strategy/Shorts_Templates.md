# 📋 Reusable Shorts Storytelling Templates

If you ever get stuck or want to deviate from the daily plan, use these proven 60-second narrative frameworks.

## Template 1: The "Struggle -> Breakthrough" (Best for hard concept days)
- **0:00 - 0:03 (Hook):** "Today I spent 4 hours trying to understand [Concept], and I almost quit."
- **0:03 - 0:15 (Context):** "It's Day [X] of becoming a Data Scientist. I was trying to build [Project] but I kept getting this error." *(Show screen recording of error)*.
- **0:15 - 0:35 (The Work):** "I read the documentation, I watched 3 tutorials, and I drew it out on a whiteboard." *(Fast montage of studying)*.
- **0:35 - 0:50 (The Breakthrough):** "Then, it finally clicked. The issue was just [Simple Explanation]." *(Show working code)*.
- **0:50 - 0:60 (Outro):** "Progress is slow but it's real. See you tomorrow for Day [Y]."

## Template 2: The "Project Showcase" (Best for milestone days)
- **0:00 - 0:03 (Hook):** "I just built an AI that can predict [Thing] from scratch."
- **0:03 - 0:10 (Context):** "I'm [X] days into my Data Science journey, and today was project day."
- **0:10 - 0:40 (The Demo):** "Here is how it works. You input [X], it runs through a [Algorithm Name] model, and outputs [Y]." *(Show highly visual demo)*.
- **0:40 - 0:55 (The Reflection):** "Looking back at Day 1, I didn't even know what a variable was. Now I'm building this."
- **0:55 - 0:60 (Call to Action):** "What should I add to this project? Let me know in the comments."

## Template 3: The "Reality Check" (Best for Flex days or burnout days)
- **0:00 - 0:05 (Hook):** "Nobody talks about how lonely learning to code actually is."
- **0:05 - 0:25 (The Truth):** "It's Day [X]. Honestly, today sucked. I had zero motivation, and I just wanted to play video games." *(Show footage of looking tired or staring blankly)*.
- **0:25 - 0:45 (The Pivot):** "But I made a promise to myself. 90 days. No excuses. So I opened my laptop and just did 30 minutes of review."
- **0:45 - 0:60 (The Takeaway):** "Consistency isn't about being motivated every day. It's about showing up when you aren't. Day [X] is done."
