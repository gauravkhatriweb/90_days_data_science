# On-Camera Dialogue Guide

**Dialogue**: 
"I stared at my screen for an hour today and understood absolutely nothing."
*(Cut to B-Roll)*
"Day 9. I finally hit my first real wall: Object-Oriented Programming. [Defeated sigh]. Classes, objects, polymorphism... I just couldn't grasp it. So, I stepped away. I made a coffee and literally drew it out on paper. [Slight smile]. And you know what? It slowly started making sense. It was brutal, but I didn't quit."

**Performance Notes**:
- Emotion: Vulnerable, then determined.
- Eye Contact: Maintain strong eye contact during the hook and the ending.
- Pauses: Let the realizations breathe. Don't rush the words.
