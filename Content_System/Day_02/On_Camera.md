# On-Camera Dialogue Guide

**Dialogue**: 
"I thought Python was supposed to be easy. I was wrong."
*(Cut to B-Roll)*
"Day 2, and I’m learning about data types. [Sigh, slight head shake]. I literally spent 45 minutes trying to figure out why the computer was yelling at me for adding a number to a word. [Pause for effect]. Pro tip: you can't do that. After a ton of Googling and feeling really dumb, I finally learned about type conversion. [Relieved exhale]. Brain is completely fried, but the code works!"

**Performance Notes**:
- Emotion: Frustration turning into relief.
- Eye Contact: Maintain strong eye contact during the hook and the ending.
- Pauses: Let the realizations breathe. Don't rush the words.
