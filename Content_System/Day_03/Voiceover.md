# Voiceover Script

*Record this in a quiet environment if the room tone is too loud during A-Roll recording. Keep it conversational.*

"If you want to be a programmer, get ready to talk to yourself a lot. Day 3, and today was all about conditional statements. [Chuckle]. It sounds easy, right? But when you nest them together, your logic can break instantly. [Look slightly off camera, recalling]. I literally had to explain my code out loud to an empty room to find my mistake. It's called rubber duck debugging... and guys, it actually works."

**Direction**: Don't read like a robot. Imagine you are leaving a voice note for a close friend explaining your day. Pause where natural.
