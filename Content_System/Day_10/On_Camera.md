# On-Camera Dialogue Guide

**Dialogue**: 
"10 days ago, I couldn't write a single line of code. Today, I built a system."
*(Cut to B-Roll)*
"Day 10. After completely struggling with OOP yesterday, I had to prove to myself that I understood it. [Confident stance]. I used constructors to build a mini project. Actually typing 'self.name' and knowing exactly what it means? [Smile]. Unbelievably rewarding. Ten days down, eighty to go. Tomorrow... the real data science begins."

**Performance Notes**:
- Emotion: Victorious and ready for the future.
- Eye Contact: Maintain strong eye contact during the hook and the ending.
- Pauses: Let the realizations breathe. Don't rush the words.
