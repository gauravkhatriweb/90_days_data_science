## 🔬 Understanding Check

The integration questions — these are harder than any single day's, because they cross topics.

1. **Integration.** You have 200,000 sales rows, 3% malformed. Describe the full pipeline from file to summary, naming the structure and technique at each step. This is `shopsummary` at scale.
2. **Reasoning.** Which of Days 1–13 will matter most once Pandas arrives on Day 32, and which will matter least? Justify both.
3. **Application.** Which single technique from these two weeks would most improve a piece of code you have already written — in LifeOS or SwiftBase? Name the file.
4. **Self-assessment.** Which topic do you *believe* you know but could not demonstrate today? That gap is the most useful thing on this page.
