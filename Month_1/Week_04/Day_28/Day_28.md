## 🎯 Objective

Establish honestly what Month 1 produced, and set Month 2 from that evidence rather than from the original plan.

---

## 📋 Today

Work through **[Month 1 Review](../../Month_1_Review.md)** in order. It has eight parts and takes about 90 minutes.

The review is built to answer one question: **what can you actually do now that you could not on 16 September?** Not what you studied, not what you watched — what you can do, with proof.

---

## 🧠 Before You Start — three rules

**1. Evidence, not memory.** Every claim needs something to point at. "I understand OOP" is not a finding. "Day 21 Part A scored 8/10 and `lifelog` uses composition with two swappable sources" is.

**2. Your Day 1 diagnostic is the baseline.** You scored yourself out of 12 on 16 September. Retake the same twelve tasks today. **The difference is the real measure of the month** — and it is the only measure that is not vulnerable to how you feel today.

**3. The review is allowed to change Month 2.** That is its purpose. If OOP is still weak, Month 2 gets a Python warm-up. If Python is strong, NumPy compresses and the extra days go to statistics. Writing a review that changes nothing is writing a diary.

---

## 📦 Completion Criteria

- [ ] Day 1 diagnostic retaken, and the two scores compared
- [ ] All eight parts of the Month 1 Review completed in writing
- [ ] Specific changes to Month 2 written down, with reasons
- [ ] The repository committed and tidy
