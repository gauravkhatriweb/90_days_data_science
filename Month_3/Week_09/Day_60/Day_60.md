## 🎯 Today's actual objective

Build something with people, under time pressure, on a problem you did not choose.

That is a different skill from anything in these ninety days, and it is one employers ask about directly. *"Tell me about a time you worked in a team under a deadline"* is a standard interview question, and after this weekend you will have a real answer to it.

---

## 🧠 Three things worth keeping in mind

**1. If there is data involved, you are more useful than you think.**
Fifty-nine days in, you can clean a messy dataset, explore it, visualise it, and put a confidence interval on a claim. Most hackathon teams have nobody who can do that. **Offer it.**

**2. Ship something that runs.**
A working rough thing beats an elegant unfinished thing. That is true at a hackathon and it is true of Project 3 in three weeks.

**3. Write down what happens.**
Not for the curriculum — for you. What the problem was, what you built, what broke, what you would do differently. It is a career artefact, and it will be far less accurate if you reconstruct it in December.
