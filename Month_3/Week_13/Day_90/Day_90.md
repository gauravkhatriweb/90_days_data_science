## 🎯 Today

Work through **[the Month 3 and 90-Day Review](../../Month_3_Review.md)**. It has eleven parts and takes about ninety minutes.

It does three things:

1. **Measures what changed** — against the Day 1 diagnostic, not against how you feel today
2. **Names what is still weak** — specifically enough to act on
3. **Sets the next ninety days** from evidence rather than from enthusiasm

---

## 🧠 Before You Start — four rules

**1. Measure, do not reminisce.** You have a Day 1 diagnostic score, weekly scores, monthly assessments, three shipped repositories and a "cannot explain" list from yesterday. **Use them.** A review written from memory on the last day will be generous in exactly the places it should not be.

**2. Judge against Day 1, not against a data scientist.** Ninety days ago: Python early, statistics zero, SQL zero, no portfolio. The question is *what changed*, not *are you finished*. You are not finished. Nobody is at day ninety.

**3. The weaknesses are the most useful output.** A review that lists only progress is a celebration. The specific list of what you still cannot do is what makes the next ninety days worth anything.

**4. Do not plan the next ninety days today.** Today is Part 11's *direction*, not a schedule. Building the next plan while still inside this one produces the same over-commitment the year-end audit already caught once. **Sleep on it. Decide in January.**
